# MonkeyHub licensing

Unless a file or third-party notice says otherwise, the first-party source code in this repository is licensed under **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`)**. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) for the license text.

## Why AGPL

MonkeyHub includes local/desktop software as well as networked Hub, agent and tool-integration paths. We want architects, researchers and developers to be able to inspect, modify and redistribute the system while keeping improvements to modified network-deployed versions available to their users.

This is why the project uses AGPL rather than a permissive license such as MIT or Apache-2.0, or ordinary GPLv3 alone. AGPL adds a source-availability obligation for modified versions that users interact with over a network.

## Commercial licensing

AGPL is an open-source license and it does **not** mean that every company must pay. Organizations may use the software under AGPL if they comply with its terms.

The copyright holders may also offer separate commercial terms for organizations that need, for example, proprietary redistribution or a modified hosted deployment without AGPL source-release obligations. A commercial license is a separate agreement; this repository does not grant those proprietary rights by default.

## Third-party software and assets

Dependencies, SDKs, models, fonts, example assets and other third-party materials retain their own licenses. Their inclusion or use does not relicense them under AGPL. Check the relevant package, vendor or file-level notices before redistribution.

## Contributions and future dual licensing

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. If MonkeyHub adopts a formal dual-licensing business model, the project should put a contributor-license or relicensing agreement in place before accepting contributions whose copyrights would otherwise prevent the maintainers from offering consistent commercial terms.
