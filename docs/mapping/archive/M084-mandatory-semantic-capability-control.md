# M084 — Mandatory semantic capability control

Status: active  
Actor: codex

## Goal

Make a declared stair semantic mechanically activate one versioned,
framework-owned rule pack, a non-dischargeable design-turn work item, and an
exact staged vertical-circulation denominator.  The project supplies evidence
and design parameters; it cannot omit, weaken, retarget, or replace the
mandatory control policy.

## Boundaries

- The framework declares questions and validation maturity, never a
  building-specific stair dimension or form.
- `SemanticCapabilityPolicy@1` is persisted through P036 and replayed against
  an exact branch/epoch/stage/subject/component binding.
- Spatial, developed, and coordinated turns require that exact inventory
  before expert discovery.  A binding creates a `SemanticDesignWorkItem@1`;
  acknowledging the item is not validation or stage acceptance.
- Every active semantic rule reference is part of both the stage requirement
  and the independent check denominator; a pack name alone cannot satisfy it.
- Stage 2 reserves exact terminal interfaces, envelope, integer step solution,
  landing ownership, and applicability basis.
- Stage 3/4 remain fail-closed until retained generic producers can prove
  walking-surface/readback and coordinated assembly claims.
- Legacy inventory/bundle schemas are read-only; they cannot author a new
  phase advance or serve as a writable same/next-epoch checkpoint anchor under
  a current proof.
- Canonical HEAD is never changed by validation or controller archive work.

## Acceptance

- A stair component automatically receives `VERTICAL_CIRCULATION / REQUIRED`
  at spatial, developed, and coordinated baseline levels.
- Its exact binding automatically creates a semantic work item, activates the
  vertical-circulation expert without relying on an ordinary state obligation,
  and requires the action to cite the work item and every active rule ref.
- Discharging an ordinary `DesignObligation` cannot remove that work item or
  satisfy the stair check; every active rule ref must remain in the requirement
  and validator-receipt denominator.
- Caller omission is filled mechanically; caller `NOT_APPLICABLE`, changed
  target, changed basis, deleted binding, unsupported policy, stale subject
  index, or cross-branch/epoch binding fails closed.
- In-memory phase advance rejects an unbound/legacy inventory.
- P036 save/reload reads the exact policy record and independently rebuilds the
  inventory; a missing or changed policy record fails without changing HEAD.
- Historical checkpoint epochs are filtered before current-epoch identity
  validation; cross-run/branch, event-chain failure, and same-epoch ambiguity
  remain rejected.
- The Villa Rotonda Stage 2 model remains a HOLD topology/reservation artifact;
  no project or canonical data is promoted by this work card.

## Verification

- Semantic capability, stair solver/geometry/vertical-circulation tests.
- Stage inventory injection and tamper tests.
- Stage baseline, relation inheritance/realization/authoring/coverage tests.
- Design controller, hierarchical search, and durable resume integration.
- `compileall`, `archcheck`, and diff audit.

## Stop conditions

- Stop before putting any Villa Rotonda dimension or geometry answer into the
  framework rule pack.
- Stop before allowing a framework rule pack to satisfy its own project
  evidence, design, validation, persistence, acceptance, or canonical-write
  requirement.
- Stop if exact P036 policy replay, legacy read-only separation, or canonical
  HEAD immutability cannot be demonstrated.
